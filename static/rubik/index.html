Notation guide

R L U D F B = turn that face 90° clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it (Right, Left, Up, Down, Front, Back).

' (e.g. R') = counter-clockwise. 2 (e.g. R2) = half turn, 180°.

r or Rw = turn the two outer layers on that side together (same for l/Lw, u/Uw...).

M E S = middle layers: M is between L and R (turns like L), E is between U and D (turns like D), S is between F and B (turns like F).

x y z = rotate the whole cube, like R, U, F respectively.
